03 / THE ONE THAT STAYED WITH ME

My Favorite Literary Masterpiece

MY CHOICE

The Odyssey

By Homer

Overview

The Odyssey is an ancient Greek epic poem traditionally attributed to Homer. It follows Odysseus on his long journey home to Ithaca after the Trojan War, as he faces dangerous trials while his wife, Penelope, and son, Telemachus, wait for his return.

Why I chose this masterpiece

I chose this epic because its adventures are exciting and its lessons about perseverance, cleverness, and loyalty make the story meaningful. Odysseus journey keeps me interested while showing how determination can help someone face difficult challenges.

Themes, characters, and lessons

Odysseus is clever and determined, while Penelope shows patience and loyalty, and Telemachus grows into courage. The poem explores homecoming, hospitality, temptation, and perseverance, reminding readers to think carefully, remain faithful, and keep going through hardship.

My personal connection

I admire the way Odysseus keeps working toward home even when the journey becomes difficult. His story reminds me that reaching an important goal takes patience, wise choices, and determination.

04 / WHAT I TAKE WITH ME

Summary of Learning

Throughout the Great Books course, I studied literary works from different periods and cultures, explored them through literary lenses, and connected their themes to human experiences and society. These activities strengthened my interpretation, analysis, and critical thinking skills.

01

Literature across cultures

Studying works from different cultures helped me understand how people express their values, beliefs, and traditions through stories. Although the settings and characters differ, many works share themes such as courage, family, justice, and loyalty.

02

Understanding literary lenses

I learned to use religious, moral, historical, sociological, and archetypal lenses to examine a work from different viewpoints. These approaches helped me better understand its characters, conflicts, themes, and cultural context.

03

Critical thinking

Analyzing the works taught me to look closely at character actions, conflicts, and details in the text before forming an interpretation. I learned to question ideas, consider different viewpoints, and support my conclusions with evidence.

04

Literature and real life

The stories connect to real life through themes such as justice, loyalty, courage, family, and the consequences of our choices. They helped me see how literature reflects the beliefs and challenges of individuals and societies across time.

“The stories we read can change the way we see the world.”

How has my appreciation of literature changed?

I used to see literature mainly as stories to read, but now I notice how it reflects people, cultures, and real human struggles. Analyzing the works helped me look for deeper meanings and lessons.

02

What challenges did I encounter?

Some works were challenging because they came from unfamiliar cultures and time periods, and their ideas were not always easy to understand at first.

03

Which literary lens influenced me the most?

The moral and ethical lens influenced me the most because it helped me think about the choices characters make and the consequences that follow. It also encouraged me to consider what is right and how our actions affect others.

04

What realizations did I gain?

I realized that people from different cultures and time periods often face similar struggles involving family, justice, loyalty, and difficult choices. Stories can help us understand perspectives beyond our own.

How will I apply what I learned?

I can apply what I learned by thinking carefully before making decisions and considering how they may affect other people. I will also try to listen to different perspectives and stay determined when I face challenges.
